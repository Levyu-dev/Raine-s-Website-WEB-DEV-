Raine's Cakes and Pastries

Welcome to Raine's Cakes and Pastries Iloilo! For a sweet tooth like you, we serve delicious cakes, pastries, and other baked goods that are perfect for any occasion. We also serve a variety of coffees and drinks to complement our delicious baked goods. If sweets weren't enough, we also offer a range of savory items to satisfy all your taste buds. Whether you crave something sweet or savory, something hot or cold, or something in between, we have the perfect treat for you. Visit us to satisfy your sweet tooth and food cravings.

About Us

Raine's Cakes and Pastries is a home-based bakery located in Iloilo City, Philippines. We specialize in creating delicious cakes, pastries, and other baked goods that are perfect for any occasion. Our mission is to provide our customers with high-quality, freshly baked treats that are made with love and care.
